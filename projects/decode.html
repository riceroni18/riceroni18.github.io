Project Overview

The DeCoDE Institute capstone focused on identifying shared extracellular RNA signals across neurodegenerative and cognitive disorders.

Our team explored patterns across multiple biological datasets and worked to interpret potential biomarker signals within the broader disease context.

The project placed 1st overall in the capstone program.

My Contribution

My primary role focused on biological research and interpretation.

I reviewed disease biology and relevant literature to help contextualize computational findings, support team discussions, and strengthen the biological interpretation presented in the final project.

I also explored differential expression analysis with DESeq2 using one project dataset as additional hands-on practice.

Research Focus

The project investigated whether extracellular RNA biomarkers could reveal biological signals shared across multiple neurodegenerative and cognitive conditions.

Extracellular RNA is particularly interesting because it may provide measurable molecular information outside of cells and has potential applications in biomarker discovery.

Our work centered on connecting computational findings with biologically meaningful interpretations.
